Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the MiniHost app for Android (package uk.jj.minihost) and this website. MiniHost is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • MiniHost is a server you run yourself. Your files, films, websites, accounts and logs stay on your phone.
  • The developer runs no servers for MiniHost and receives nothing from it. There is no advertising and no analytics.
  • MiniHost only goes online for the features you switch on yourself: outside access, alerts, film posters, backups and the update check.

What stays on your phone

The files you share stay where they are on the phone, SD card or USB drive. MiniHost keeps its own settings, accounts (passwords are stored only as salted hashes), share links, website list, resume points, activity log and the server's certificate in its private storage. Android's cloud backup is switched off for MiniHost.

People who use your server

You run the server, so you are responsible for the people you give accounts or links to. MiniHost keeps an activity log on the phone (who signed in, from which address, and which files were opened) so you can see what is happening. It is never sent anywhere.

Keeping and deleting your data

Delete accounts, share links and websites in the app or the web admin. Uninstalling MiniHost, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes its settings, accounts, links and logs. Neither touches the files you shared.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you or the people who use your server, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server of ours. Everything is in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the app list. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
