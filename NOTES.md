MiniHost 1.0.0: turn a spare Android phone into your own private file, website and media server. Android 10 and later.

- Files in any browser with resumable uploads, previews and editing; a network drive (WebDAV) and SFTP; share links with expiry, password and download limits.
- Accounts for admin, family and guests, with 6-digit codes for admins and the padlock (HTTPS) on every connection.
- Websites from four ready-made designs or your own zip, and a Netflix-style library of films, shows and music with resume, subtitles and smart-TV streaming.
- Home only by default. Switch on Anywhere to reach chosen services through Tailscale or Cloudflare.
- Alerts on your main phone through ntfy, a Healthchecks.io gone-offline check, a one-tap health check and speed test, and encrypted daily backups to Google Drive.
- Server Mode, an illustrated set-up walkthrough and a searchable help centre.
